About the underlying data

mineral.watch is built on open data published by the British Geological Survey, the U.S. Geological Survey, UN Comtrade, the IEA, the World Bank, the World Nuclear Association, the IAEA and the US EIA, among others. Those figures are theirs, not ours, and we make no ownership claim over them.

What we do claim is the work of turning them into this: the selection of what matters, the cleaning and cross-checking, the geocoding of facilities, the structuring, and the analysis wrapped around it. That compilation is protected in its own right — including as a database right under Directive 96/9/EC and equivalent law — and it is what the CC BY-NC-SA licence above covers.

If you want the raw numbers with no strings attached, go to the original publishers. That is the honest route and we will happily point you to the right dataset.
