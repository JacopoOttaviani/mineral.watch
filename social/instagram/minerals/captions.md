```
Graphite: who controls the anode?

The biggest material in an EV battery isn't lithium.

82% of the world’s natural graphite was mined in China in 2025. (USGS MCS 2026)
~50 kg of graphite in every electric car: the largest material by weight in a lithium-ion battery. (USGS · IEA)

China refines ~90% of battery-grade graphite and ~95% of the spherical graphite used in anodes. The US imports 100% of the natural graphite it uses. Reserves are ample at ~310 Mt; the chokepoint is processing, not geology.
```

## Cobalt: whose hands power the battery age?

- **Link:** https://mineral.watch/cobalt
- **Files:** `cobalt/slide-01.jpg`, `cobalt/slide-02.jpg`, `cobalt/slide-03.jpg`, `cobalt/slide-04.jpg`

**Carousel caption**

```
Cobalt: whose hands power the battery age?

Nearly all cobalt is a by-product. Most of it comes from one country.

73% of the world’s cobalt was mined in the DR Congo in 2025. (USGS MCS 2026)
~79% of the world’s refined cobalt came from China in 2025. (Cobalt Institute)

Since October 2025 the DRC caps exports at 96,600 t a year, about half of 2024 volumes. Some 150,000–250,000 artisanal miners dig cobalt by hand. Cobalt-free LFP batteries already power more than half of new EVs.
```

## Rare earths: who controls the magnets?

- **Link:** https://mineral.watch/rare-earths

```
Rare earths: who controls the magnets?

17 elements, and one supplier for the magnets in every motor and missile.

94% of sintered NdFeB magnets are made in China. (IEA)
7 of 17 rare earth elements have been under Chinese export controls since April 2025. (MOFCOM · USGS)

China handles ~91% of rare earth refining and separation. US import reliance is 67% overall and 100% for heavy rare earths. Global reserves exceed 75 Mt; the constraint is separation capacity, not geology.
```

## Manganese: who hardens the world's steel?

- **Link:** https://mineral.watch/manganese
- **Files:** `manganese/slide-01.jpg`, `manganese/slide-02.jpg`, `manganese/slide-03.jpg`, `manganese/slide-04.jpg`

**Carousel caption**

```
Manganese: who hardens the world's steel?

Ninety percent goes into steel, and nothing else does the job.

95% of battery-grade manganese sulphate is refined in China. (IEA)
4% of the world’s manganese ore is mined in China. South Africa mines ~38%. (USGS MCS 2026)

USGS lists no satisfactory substitute for manganese in steelmaking. Gabon bans raw ore exports from 2029. Battery demand for manganese is projected to grow more than eightfold this decade.
```

## Antimony: who's playing with fire?

```
Antimony: who's playing with fire?

A small market with one of the thinnest reserve cushions of any strategic mineral.

>85% of the world’s antimony is mined in China, Russia and Tajikistan. (USGS MCS 2026)
$60,000 /t: the spring-2025 price peak after China began licensing exports, up from ~$12,000–14,000. (price-agency assessments)

Reserves of ~2 Mt are under two decades of supply at current mining rates. Antimony goes into flame retardants, lead-acid batteries, solar glass, ammunition primers and night-vision detectors.
```
